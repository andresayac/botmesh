Histograms get bucket boundaries that fit their unit (instrument advice): 5 ms to 10 s for the span-derived latencies, the GenAI semantic-convention buckets for `gen_ai.client.operation.duration` and `gen_ai.client.token.usage`, and 1 s to 1 day for `botmesh.conversation.duration`. Before, they used the SDK defaults (0 to 10000, meant for milliseconds), so every duration in seconds fell in the first bucket. Span metrics now honour `startTimeMs`/`endTimeMs` without mixing clocks: a span with no caller-given time is measured with the monotonic clock, and one with `startTimeMs` or `endTimeMs` reads both ends on the wall clock (`Date.now()`), so a wall-clock step never lands in a duration; a negative duration records 0. An invalid metric `prefix` (or `spanMetrics.prefix`) throws a clear `TypeError` instead of producing instruments the SDK only warns about, and `otelMetricsPlugin()` without an injected `api` logs a bridge that fails to register instead of raising an unhandled rejection. The guides use `metricReaders: [reader]`, which current `@opentelemetry/sdk-node` releases require.
